How to read this

  • FAKE = positive class. Precision = of flagged-fake, how many truly fake. Recall = of truly-fake, how many caught.
  • HQ-test is the original distribution (COCO reals + MJ/nano/art fakes). This checkpoint was later retrained on AUTO, so HQ FAKE recall 0.818 shows mild forgetting vs its original 0.947 val — reported honestly.
  • AUTO-test covers modern generators (Defactify SD3/DALLE3/MJv6, v3 FLUX, Tiny-GenImage) — the closest thing to an unseen-generator check without a 12GB Synthbuster download.
  • Threshold is fixed at 0.5 (see config.THRESHOLD). No threshold tuning was applied — F1/AUC are reported at 0.5.
  • Regenerate anytime: python eval_metrics.py --checkpoint weights/hq_efficientnet_b0.pt
