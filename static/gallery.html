Dataset references

Every image below comes from one of these sources. All three sets are balanced REAL==FAKE and canonicalized to 512px JPEG Q95.

HQ · 4,000 · live brain

Built in-house: COCO val2017 reals (Lin et al., 2014) + NEWER fakes filtered to ≥224px, reals ≥448px. Train 1500+1500, test 500+500.

Refs: COCO (cocodataset.org) · GenImage, Zhu et al. 2023 (MidJourney shard) · julienlucas midjourney-dalle-sd-nanobanana mix · hmnshudhmn24 art-images

NEWER · ~14.5k · lineage

CIFAKE (Bird & Lotfi, 2024) + MidJourney / DALL-E / SD / NanoBanana / art fakes. Train 5770+5770, test 1472+1472. Staging pool — no checkpoint trains on it directly.

Refs: CIFAKE (Kaggle birdy654) · bitmind/GenImage_MidJourney · julienlucas/…-nanobanapro · hmnshudhmn24/…-art-images

AUTO · ~6k · modern gens

Streamed via dl_auto.py (SHA256 dedup, resume-safe). Train ~2500+2500, test 498+498. Retrains hq_*.pt.

Refs: Rajarshi-Roy-research/Defactify_Image_Dataset (SD2.1/SDXL/SD3/DALLE3/MJv6, 96k) · Shanmuk4622/ai-detection-dataset-v3 (SD1.5/SDXL/FLUX/Kandinsky/PixArt/Würstchen, 70k) · TheKernel01/Tiny-GenImage (ADM/BigGAN/GLIDE/MJ/SD1.4/1.5/VQDM/Wukong)

Why this matters: CIFAKE was 32px single-generator (resolution shortcut). NEWER added generators. HQ keeps only ≥224px fakes + ≥448px COCO reals, 512px Q95, REAL==FAKE. AUTO adds modern generators (SD3, DALL-E 3, FLUX, MJv6) the same way.
